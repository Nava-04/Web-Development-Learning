Apple

Apples are composed primarily of water (86%), carbohydrates (14%), and natural sugars (fructose, sucrose, glucose). They also pack essential dietary fiber (like pectin), Vitamin C, and beneficial plant antioxidants (such as quercetin and chlorogenic acid) that support immune health.

Banana

Bananas are rich in potassium, vitamin B6, and dietary fiber. They also contain natural sugars like fructose, glucose, and sucrose, making them a quick source of energy.
